# C-berry — single-page spa website

One-page site for **C-berry**, a 24-hour spa inside De Castle Hotel, G.R.A., Enugu.
Built with Vite + React + TypeScript. No router: the header links scroll to sections on the page.

**Sections:** Hero → Treatments → Gallery → Reviews → Book a session / Find us → Footer.
The reviews currently on the page are **placeholders, not real guests** — replacing them is a launch
blocker. Email and social links are still deferred: see
[`tickets/LATER-001`](tickets/LATER-001-contact-socials-reviews.md).
Nail Service is not offered yet and is out of the build: see [`tickets/LATER-002`](tickets/LATER-002-nail-service.md).

---

## Run it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev       # local dev server
npm run build     # type-check and build to dist/
npm run preview   # serve the production build locally
npm run images    # re-process photos after adding or swapping one
```

Deploy the `dist/` folder to any static host (Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, S3).

## Motion

Direction: [`design-plan-motion.md`](design-plan-motion.md), amended by
[`c-berry-motion-amendment.md`](c-berry-motion-amendment.md) (§6b), which raised the amplitude after
the first build proved imperceptible at normal scrolling speed. Frames of each section 0, 300 and
900ms into its reveal are in [`docs/motion-frames/`](docs/motion-frames/).

- **One gesture per section.** Every section heading wipes up out of a mask with its supporting line
  a beat behind. Treatments: each card wipes up while its photo settles from 1.10, price line last,
  110ms apart. Gallery: tiles uncover along their long edge in diagonal waves, photos settling inside.
  Reviews: the featured quote is uncovered line by line, cards rise 48px beneath it, stars fill left
  to right. Booking panel: one unit from 64px away. Footer: its top rule draws across, then the
  columns come up.
- **Every photo drifts in its frame as you scroll**, the hero's parallax applied to all images, and
  the gallery's two halves drift against each other. One scroll listener and one rAF for all of it.
- **Touch is a first-class input.** Phones have no hover, so the card or tile crossing the middle of
  the screen takes the hover state, and presses give under the finger.
- **The hero is a beat sheet**: the photo settles from 1.10 while the headline is uncovered line by
  line, then the sub-line, CTAs, facts and header. 1200ms end to end; the last text lands by ~960ms.
  Timings live in `HERO_BEATS` in [`src/lib/motion.ts`](src/lib/motion.ts).
- **Reveals fire once**, when each unit is 15% into the viewport, never replay, and play at 0.6x if
  the unit is already past mid-screen when it fires. On a phone, cards arrive one at a time as they
  appear rather than waiting on stagger slots meant for their neighbours.
- **The header is scroll-linked**: alpha and blur interpolate over the first 100px, with a 0.72 alpha
  floor that keeps the nav at 6.1:1 over any part of the hero photo.
- **The lightbox uses a FLIP transition**, flying back to whichever photo you navigated to.
- **One loop on the whole page**: the "open now" dot, because it reports ongoing state.
- **No animation library.** CSS keyframes plus one module of JavaScript for triggers, line
  measurement, drift and the FLIP.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion` is structural.** Every hidden state lives inside a `no-preference`
  media query; under reduce, nothing is hidden, nothing drifts, and no animation runs.

## Images

Every photo runs through `scripts/process-images.mjs`, which:

- makes WebP copies at up to 480/800/1200/1600px wide (never enlarged);
- gives cooler photos a candle-coloured grade so stock and in-house shots match;
- writes their `src`, `srcset`, `width`, `height` and `alt` into `src/data/imageManifest.json`.

To swap a photo, change its `file` in `SOURCES` and run `npm run images`.

`assets/Nail_Service.jpg` is still in `assets/` but unused: nails aren't offered yet. To bring the
treatment back, re-add its `SOURCES` entry and its `services` entry, then run `npm run images`.

## Booking form

- Fields: name, email (optional), phone/WhatsApp, treatment, date, time, guests, notes.
- Each field is checked when the visitor leaves it and again on submit. Dates and times are checked in Enugu time, and any hour is allowed because C-berry is open 24/7.
- Every "Book …" button on a treatment pre-selects that treatment and scrolls to the form.
- While the Formspree endpoint is empty, a valid submit shows a panel offering to send the same details as a pre-filled WhatsApp message. Nothing is lost.

## Acceptance rubric (brief §7): self-score

Weights are the renormalised set from the motion spec, with Motion quality added as row 10.

Scored after an independent production review (Fable), fixes (Sonnet), the motion build, and a live
re-check in headless Chrome: five widths (320/768/1024/1440/1920), scroll traces at each, a
`prefers-reduced-motion` pass, and frame-rate measurement under CPU throttling.

| # | Criterion | Weight | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | **Motion quality** (§6b) | 15% | 4 | Everything §6a asked for still holds, plus the §6b bar that can be measured: every section including Reviews and the footer arrives with motion, no two share a gesture, every photo reveal is two-speed, each section's 0ms and 300ms frames are plainly different (see `docs/motion-frames/`), and 58.4fps under 4× CPU throttling with no long tasks. Held at 4, not 5, because §6b's deciding criterion — a viewer scrolling at normal speed can describe each section unprompted — is a human judgement the frames can't settle. Frame rate is from throttled headless Chrome, not a real mid-range Android. |
| 1 | Fidelity to reference | 15% | 4 | Same dark warm ground, fluid type scale, screen-scaled gutters, square ghost buttons, 12px image radius and slow-settling easing as remedyplace.com, now with a scroll-linked header and hero parallax. The hero is split rather than full-bleed because every supplied photo is portrait, and there are no page transitions because there are no pages. |
| 2 | Brand distinctness | 12% | 5 | Candlelight palette plus Berry from the name, spa italics, 24/7 "open now" clock with the page's only looping animation, WhatsApp-first contact, naira prices up front; no borrowed copy or marks. |
| 3 | Section completeness | 12% | 5 | All seven sections now present and in order — Reviews was the gap and is built. Every §5 field implemented. Reviews content is placeholder and marked as such; email and socials stay omitted rather than stubbed, which §3 requires when no URL was supplied. |
| 4 | Responsiveness | 8% | 5 | Measured zero horizontal overflow and every section revealing correctly at all five widths, and the rendered pages were looked at this time, not just measured. |
| 5 | Image handling | 8% | 4 | Fallback chain followed, one colour grade, alt/width/height/lazy on every image, source table above, and photographs now fade in as they decode instead of popping. Six stock placeholders and four low-resolution owner photos remain. |
| 6 | Form correctness | 8% | 5 | Inline errors, first-invalid focus, service pre-select, disabled submit while pending, plus success, failure and not-configured states — all four paths driven end to end against a stubbed endpoint, in both motion settings. |
| 7 | Accessibility | 10% | 5 | AA contrast (computed, including the header's new 0.72 alpha floor at 6.1:1), one h1, landmarks, labelled fields, full keyboard traversal including the lightbox, focus hand-off on every section link, hover states matched by `focus-visible`, reduced motion verified end to end. | 8 | Code quality | 6% | 5 | Tokenised CSS, one folder per section, one motion module holding every timing, no dead rules (the old `--ease-out-expo`, `--dur-fast`, `--dur-base` and `.is-scrolled` are gone), typed data as the single source. |
| 9 | Copywriting | 6% | 5 | Plain, specific to a 24-hour spa in G.R.A. Enugu; buttons name the action. Review copy is written to sound like Enugu guests and is labelled as placeholder wherever it appears. |

**Changed as a result of the motion build:** the Reviews section was built rather than deferred; the
hero's old 2.8s candle warm-up and 120ms group stagger were replaced with the beat sheet (both

opening the lightbox no longer shifts the page under the photo the FLIP is flying from.
